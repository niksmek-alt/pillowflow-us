Quick answers

Answers to common questions.

Can't find your issue? Contact us directly. You never have to work through a long menu.

How do I access my driver pilot?

Open the invitation email from PillowFlow and use its sign-in link. You can also visit the pilot portal. If your link expired or you cannot access the assigned pilot, contact our team using the driver-pilot option above.

My survey is missing. What should I do?

Make sure you signed in with the email address used for your invitation. If the survey still does not appear, contact pilot support. Include your fleet name and the survey you expected to see; do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

Where can I find installation instructions?

Use the installation video. Only review instructions or adjust the product when safely parked. Stop using it if it interferes with safe vehicle operation.

How do I contact someone about my fleet pilot?

Email connect@pillowflow.com with your company name and the issue. Our team can follow up about invitations, rollout, participation, and other pilot questions.

Can I reach support while I'm driving?

Do not read or send messages, complete surveys, or adjust the product while operating a vehicle. Stop in a safe, permitted location before contacting support. This page is not an emergency or medical service.
