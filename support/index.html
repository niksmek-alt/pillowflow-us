Quick answers

Common questions, answered.

If your question is not here, contact support directly.

How do I access my driver pilot?

Open your PillowFlow invitation email and use its sign-in link, or go to the pilot portal. If you cannot sign in, email support with your fleet name. Never send sign-in codes.

My survey isn't showing. What should I do?

Check that you signed in with the email used for your invitation. If the survey is still missing, tell support your fleet name and which survey you expected to see.

Where are the product setup instructions?

Watch the setup video while parked. Stop using the product if it interferes with vehicle controls or safe driving.

How do I request a return or report a damaged order?

Email support@pillowflow.com with your order number and issue. For return eligibility and deadlines, review the published refund policy.

Can I contact support while driving?

No. Do not read or send messages or adjust the product while operating a vehicle. Pull over and park safely before contacting us. This website is not an emergency or medical service.
